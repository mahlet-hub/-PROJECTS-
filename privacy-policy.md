# Privacy Policy for Learn Chess

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Thank you for using Learn Chess . Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices. If you have any questions, please contact us at: mahletgetu05@gmail.com.

## Summary
- We collect minimal data necessary to operate the App and improve your experience.
- We do not sell your personal data.
- We use system services (e.g., Game Center) only if you choose to use them.
- You can delete your data by removing the App and/or contacting us.

## Information We Collect

### 1) Information You Provide
- Profile name or username you enter within the App (stored locally on your device).

### 2) Automatically Collected Information
- Basic usage information (e.g., app screens and actions) may be collected in aggregate to improve the App’s features and performance. If analytics are used, they are anonymized and not used for tracking across apps.

### 3) Game Center (Optional)
If you sign in to Apple Game Center, Apple may share your Game Center nickname, leaderboard scores, and achievements with the App, according to Apple’s policies. Game Center is provided by Apple; please see Apple’s privacy policy for details.

## How We Use Information
- To provide core functionality (e.g., gameplay, saved games, difficulty settings).
- To improve the App’s performance, reliability, and user experience.
- To show your progress and statistics (locally or via Apple services you opt into).

## Data Storage and Retention
- Most data (e.g., settings, local progress) is stored on your device.
- If you remove the App, locally stored data is deleted from your device.
- If we collect anonymized analytics, we retain them only as long as necessary for the stated purposes.

## Sharing of Information
We do not sell your personal data. We may share limited data:
- With Apple services you choose to use (e.g., Game Center), governed by Apple’s terms and privacy policy.
- As required by law or to protect our rights and users’ safety.

## Children’s Privacy
Learn Chess is suitable for a general audience. If you are under the age required by your local law to consent to data processing, you should use the App only with a parent or guardian’s permission.

## Your Choices and Controls
- You can disable Game Center in iOS settings or choose not to sign in.
- You can reset or remove local data by deleting and reinstalling the App.
- You may request deletion of any server-side data (if any) by contacting us.

## International Transfers
If any data is processed outside your country, we take steps to ensure appropriate protections are in place consistent with applicable laws.

## Security
We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy from time to time. We will update the “Last updated” date above and, where required, notify you within the App.
